1. What the Project Is
The project is a simple inventory and profit management app designed for petty traders and small business owners. It allows traders to record their products, track stock levels, enter cost and selling prices, record sales, and automatically calculate their profit.

The goal is to replace manual calculations and paper-based stock records with a simple digital tool that helps traders understand their business finances.

2. Who It Is Designed For

The app is designed primarily for petty traders and small businesses, including:

Provision and grocery sellers
Market traders
Food and drinks sellers
Cosmetics sellers
Clothing sellers
Phone accessory sellers
Small wholesalers
Home-based businesses

It is particularly useful for traders who may not have access to, or need for, complex accounting software.
